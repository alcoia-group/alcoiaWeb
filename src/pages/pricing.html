Regional pricing

Built for students where $59 is a lot of money

The primary market for Reader is students, including in countries where $59/year is a real sum. A student discount of 40% off Reader is available with an academic email address, applied on request, and regional pricing is available for Africa, South Asia and Latin America through the payment processor. This isn't a footnote — it's who the product is priced for.

What we never charge for

Dyslexia mode, the focus ruler, text-to-speech and bionic reading are free forever

That's a commitment, not an introductory offer. It costs almost no revenue and it's the part of the product we're least willing to gate.

Questions

Do I need a webcam?

No. Reading detection runs on browser telemetry by default and works without one.

Is my video uploaded?

No. Video never leaves your device, whether or not the camera is on.

Can my school see my data?

Only if you submit it. Per-student receipts reach an instructor solely when that student chooses to submit one. Aggregate class views are anonymous.

What happens when I cancel?

Paid features stop at the end of the billing period you already paid for; the free tier keeps working. You can export your data at any time — see the refund policy for the full terms.

What's your refund policy?

Covered in full at /legal/refunds, including EU distance-selling withdrawal rights.

Can I get my data out?

Yes — export is available on request and is part of what cancelling honours. Details in the privacy policy.
