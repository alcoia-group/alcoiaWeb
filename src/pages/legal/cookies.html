This site does not set non-essential cookies

alcoia.com does not use advertising cookies, tracking cookies, or third-party embeds that set cookies. If analytics are enabled, they run on a cookieless model (Plausible or Umami) that does not identify you across visits or sites, which is why there's no cookie banner here. the absence of one is the accurate state, not an oversight.

The browser extension is separate

This policy covers the marketing website only. The alcoia browser extension uses chrome.storage, which is not a cookie and is not shared with this site; it's covered in the privacy policy instead.
