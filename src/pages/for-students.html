The receipt

Something to show for having actually read it

Press a shortcut and alcoia builds a record of what you covered and what you actually recalled, built on your own machine and shown to you before anything happens with it. It can be signed, which means it's unaltered since it was issued — nothing more. It isn't proof the reading happened; the figures come from your own browser, the same way any self-reported study log is. It's useful for the same reason a workout log is useful: not as evidence for someone else, but as a record you can trust for yourself.
