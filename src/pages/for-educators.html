For educators

Proof you actually read it.

Students opt in and submit evidence. This is not something you install on a class to watch it. It's something a student chooses to hand you.

alcoia does not let you monitor a student without their action. There is no covert mode and there never will be.

What a receipt proves: that the file is unaltered since alcoia issued it.

What it does not prove: that the reading happened. The figures inside come from the student's own browser. A modified extension could produce anything. Treat a receipt the way you'd treat any self-reported log: useful context, not forensic proof.

What you see

  • Which paragraphs the class struggled with (aggregate and anonymous, always)
  • Per-student receipts, but only where that student chose to submit one
  • Nothing from a student who hasn't opted in and submitted
